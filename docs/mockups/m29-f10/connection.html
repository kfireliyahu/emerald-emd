The connection window, in plain words

The window that opens after you drag between two formats on Connections. It now says what will happen the way you'd say it out loud: each work day belongs to one paycheck. One headline, a few short questions, and the details folded away. Nothing it could do before is gone.

Theme

The ports: what you drag from

Today the two green ports say names a row of… and a total of…. Both describe how it is stored. Two wordings for what you'd mean instead, on the same piece of the graph. A line now ends in a fork at the "many" end and a bar at the "one" end.

A · recommended One-to-many, said outright

Paycheck paycheck
Work day work_day

Read across the line: a work day belongs to one paycheck; a paycheck has many work days. Either port starts the same connection. has many… opens the window with "add up" already open, because that's usually why you started from that end.

B Verbs, closer to today's two jobs

Paycheck paycheck
Work day work_day

Short, and a verb (docs/11). But link to… doesn't say which side is the "many", so the direction is still something you learn by trying, which is the problem today.

Beside the graph: every connection, in words
Each work day belongs to one paycheckwork day's paycheck column

Today this list prints work_day.paycheck → paycheck and paycheck.earnings ← work_day.paycheck under the heading "in words".

Your call: A or B.

I recommend A, belongs to one… and has many…. It names the relationship rather than the storage, it says which end is the "many" before you drag, and the window's headline then just finishes the sentence the port started.

The layout is the same every time: a small one-to-many line, a headline that says what will be true, a few short questions, and the name and note folded under Name and note. The headline changes as you answer. Work days and paychecks, plus a recipe for a total that isn't money. Try the menus and toggles.

Notes for the build

  • Nothing is lost. Link, total, found by (month or same day), required, the unit of a non-money total, the formula with its suggestions, latest/earliest by an order, the column name and the note are all still here. Name and note are folded away.
  • The port wording is your choice (A or B above). Under A, has many… is today's a total of… port. Dropped where there is no link yet, it proposes the link with "add up" open, so un-ticking "add up" leaves a plain link. Dropped where a link exists, it goes straight to state 4.
  • The headline is built from the answers. It follows the found-by choice, the "add up" tick and the kind of total, so it always says what will be true.
  • Menus are the app's own list (ChoiceField's look, D-290), not the platform <select> the panel uses today.
  • No refusal before you've typed. A total opens with a name already suggested from what it adds up, and the button stays off quietly until it can save.
  • A refusal from the document stays word for word (D-138), folded under a plain headline that the window writes for each kind of refusal.
