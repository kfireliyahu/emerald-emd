What is clunky today

Tried on the same twelve-column task format in the app on 2026-10-22: the entry page, its Arrange mode, and Add.

  1. Every column is drawn, filled or not. A task with five values shows twelve fields: done_on as an empty mm/dd/yyyy box, blocked_by as “Type to find one”, assignee as a heading over nothing, and “One more” after the tags. Most of the page is empty fields.
  2. Raw column names are the labels: DONE_ON, BLOCKED_BY, ESTIMATE_H, with the underscores.
  3. One optional figure gets a section of its own. estimate_h sits alone under a rule as a large “3 h”, with a wide gap under it, because figures are a part of their own.
  4. Two date formats on one screen. The list beside it says “Sat 24 Oct 2026”, while the entry’s date inputs say 10/24/2026 and show mm/dd/yyyy when they are empty (the platform’s date input).
  5. Arrange is a mode. The icon turns every field into a grip with ↑ and ↓ arrows. The line under the title reflows from four fields a row to three, and it has no way to hide a field. The order holds only inside a part (the line, figures, texts), because the part comes from the column’s type. So dragging estimate_h above state changes nothing you can see.
  6. Adding takes three steps and loses the list. From an open entry: ← Task, Add, fill it in, Add. entry/new drops the list that was beside the entry and shows every column, plus a “Links here” that says nothing can link yet.
  7. It scolds before you start. The new entry opens with “Nothing has been filled in yet.” in red beside Add. The required state starts “Empty”, so even a task with only a title needs a menu opened.
  8. No view suits a task. A task gets the table, the board or the entry. There is no tick, and nothing reads “due tomorrow”. The list beside an entry is good (title, dot, state, date) but cannot be ticked, grouped or added to.

Words new on screen

Checked against the term rows. Each becomes a term row when its unit lands.

WordCovers exactly
ListThe view kind: one row per line, with a tick, the title and the line’s columns, grouped by one column. It sits beside Table and Board in the one tab strip.
FieldsThe entry’s list of every column: its order on this entry view, whether it is shown, and whether a new entry asks for it. It replaces “Arrange this view”.
EmptyThe line at the foot of an entry naming the columns that hold no value and are not hidden.
N hiddenThe columns hidden on this entry view, whether they hold a value or not. Pressing it shows them in place, dimmed, until the entry is left.
Add a task · valuesQuick add’s placeholder. It names every value the new row will take besides its title (the group’s, the filter’s, a required column’s first member), so nothing is taken silently.
N doneThe ticked rows of a group, folded at its foot.
Group by / Ticked when / LineThe list’s three settings, set in place above the list: the column the groups come from, the member or boolean that ticks a row, and the columns after the title.
