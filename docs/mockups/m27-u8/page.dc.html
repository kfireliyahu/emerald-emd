Question

How are the roadmap and the decision page declared and drawn on D-301’s view mechanism, and how far do they read?

Answer

roadmap and page join VIEW_KINDS in format_view_column, with no migration. A roadmap keeps order (the number column), under (fold another format’s rows under the row they name) and line (columns after a row’s name). Any format with a number that another format links to has one.

A self-link never folds; rows naming no root are drawn under “Under none of these”; a row whose state is its enum’s last member starts folded shut. Every row has a page: body columns in full, line columns beside the name, and what it names and what names it, one hop each way (D-224).

Nothing is kept: both re-read the document on every revision. Reads only, so no parity entry.
