The figure colours, in both themes

Came in is green, went out is red, saved is a darker green, taxes are orange. Left over stays ink: it is the difference, not a flow. The swatches below are the same figures in light and dark, whatever theme the page is in.

How this squares with D-125. D-125 says accent marks what you can act on, positive reports what the app found, and accent never colours a figure. The last part stays exactly as it is. What changes is docs/11's line that outflows are plain ink, not red: on the figures that are one of these four flows, the flow's colour is the figure's colour, and always beside the word that names it (Came in, Went out, Saved, Taxes), so colour is never the only carrier. Red stops meaning only "a problem" on these screens, so a problem keeps the form it already has: a chip with a dot and a word. Saved's green is set apart from the emerald accent by lightness (light 1.95:1 between them, dark 1.76:1) and by role, since a figure is never a fill, a border or an underline. All four pass AA as text: light 5.3, 5.4, 9.6 and 5.0 to 1; dark 9.6, 6.1, 5.9 and 9.2 to 1. When this is built, the amendment is recorded as its own decision.
