Net worth is one table

Today Wealth shows a bank account three or four times: in Balances, in In the bank: the goals saved there, and the cushion, in Where the rest is, and again under Edit places and holdings, where every bank account also had a place of its own. Here there is one row per account or place, with its figure. A row opens to what is in it: the statement and its opening balance, each goal saved there with its part, Cash, not for any goal, and any other holding. The cushion's ticks are the first column. Adding an account or a place, and merging two accounts, happen on the table. The table ends on Net worth, the same figure the top of the screen, Home and the savings tool read (D-383). Every name and figure here is made up.

Theme
a

The table, nothing open

Click any row to open it. Untick Second Bank and the cushion line below the table changes: the ticks say which accounts the cushion lives in, as BankSplit's did.
b

A bank account opened: Everyday Bank

Its statement and opening balance (what Balances showed), the two goals saved in it with their parts, and Cash, not for any goal, which is the statement less those parts (D-376). The three lines add up to the row's figure.
c

A bank account with a deposit beside it, and goals holding more than the statement: Second Bank

A fixed deposit at the bank is not in the statement, so it is a line of its own and the row is part reachable. Here a goal holds more than the 28 Sep statement ends at: the cash line says none, and the check says by how much. Never a negative, never shrunk to fit.
d

An account that holds things, and a place kept by hand, opened

Brokerage's holdings, each with its latest reading and growth, and its own cash. Cash at home is a place: money with no statement, written down by hand.
e

Adding an account, and adding a place, on the table

Both open in place under the table. A new bank account is a row at once, with nothing in it until a statement is read; a new place asks the same four kinds the goal form's + New… asks (D-363).
f

Merging two accounts: Old Second Bank into Second Bank

One account read twice, under two names, is two rows and counts twice: Net worth here is too high by Old Second Bank's ₪7,920.00. From the opened row's Merge into…; the sentence counts what moves before anything is written, as the Accounts screen's merge does.

For the author to judge

  • 1. The top of the screen is Net worth and its four parts, not four equal cards. The four parts are the ones the savings tool prints (D-383): In the bank, At accounts that hold things, Locked, Owed. They add up to the figure, and the table's last line is the same figure. In funds and You owe as cards go; funds are inside Locked, and their own rows are in the table. Bank money less debt goes. The other way: no hero at all, only the table's last line — less to read, but the one number people come for is at the bottom.
  • 2. Funds and debts are rows of the table too. So the table adds up to Net worth with nothing outside it. A fund row opens on its own page, as today; a debt row on Debts. Cards are not rows: a card holds no money, and what it charges is in the bank account that pays it. The other way: the table is accounts and places only, and funds and debts stay as lines under it.
  • 3. The cushion is a column of ticks and one line, not a section with a bar. The striped bar and its parts go: each goal's part is now a line inside its account, which is where it is. What is left is the line under the table, with the months chooser and Above / Below the cushion. The other way: keep the bar above the table as the cushion's picture.
  • 4. Adding a card stays on the Accounts screen. Add an account here offers Bank account, Cash and Holds things. A card would be a row with nothing in it. Merging two cards also stays there; merging two bank accounts, or two accounts that hold things, is in the opened row.
  • 5. A place an account's statement shows is that account's row. A fixed deposit at Second Bank is a line inside Second Bank's row, not a place of its own. Add a place is only for money with no statement — cash at home, a deposit kept elsewhere, a fund. Your existing places stay as they are; a place that holds an account's cash already reads as that account's row (D-384).

Notes for the build

  • The rows are documentMoneyMap(db, currency).places — since D-384 an account whose own cash is in no place is a row with id account:<id> — plus the bank accounts with no cash holding (accountBalances), the funds and the debts. A row's figure is the place's balance (the statement for an account's row, D-376); the last line is netWorth(db, asOf, currency).worth, and a test holds the rows' sum to it.
  • An opened account reads row.statement (balance, parts, cash, short) and accountBalances' opening, moved and check; Change is setOpeningBalance, as AccountsSection does. A place's holdings are row.holdings with HoldingReadings' New figure.
  • The ticks are setCushionAccounts; the cushion line is cushionReading's split and Months, moved out of BankSplit, which goes with its bar.
  • Adding is createAccount (no card) and makePlace from goalWhereRepository; merging is mergePreview and mergeAccounts, the Accounts screen's. Parity: no new write, so no new tool.
  • Goes: AccountsSection, BankSplit, Where the rest is, and the embedded MoneyMap with #/wealth/places (redirect it to #/wealth; holdingCheck's link opens the row instead).
