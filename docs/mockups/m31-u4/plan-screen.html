The Plan screen shows its months and how they went

Three changes. Months on this plan is a row of checkboxes, one for each month, so a month gets a plan in one click and you can see which plan each month has. Use for October… goes: the checkbox does its job, and a line about deposits and payments only appears when there is something to record. The comparison is always on the screen, and it is the bar itself: each bucket fills from the left in its own colour up to what it got in the month you pick. The plan from the 50/30/20 start still reads as 50 / 30 / 20, with its buckets grouped under Needs, Wants and Saving. Every name and figure here is made up.

For the author to judge

  • 1. Checkboxes in a row of twelve months, not a menu for each month. One click per month, the state can be seen at a glance, and under each box is the name of the plan the month is on, so the row also answers which month has which plan. A menu per month would show every plan at once but costs two clicks and hides the answer behind a closed menu. The row runs six months back and five ahead of the month on screen, and the month pager in the header moves it.
  • 2. The default still covers every month you have not given another plan. So a month is never without a budget, and on the default plan its boxes are ticked and cannot be unticked (you take a month off the default by ticking it on another plan). The other way: a month has a plan only when you tick it, and an unticked month shows no budget on Home. That adds a step every new month, so it is not drawn.
  • 3. A month keeps its plan when the plan is edited. Today any edit to a plan silently clears the month it was given, and a plan can hold only one month. Both go: a month's plan is a plain choice, kept until you change it. That needs a small table of month to plan, which replaces the one month a plan holds now (a schema change).
  • 4. The fill is the month under Against, not the average of the last three months. The dashed ticks go. Against starts on the last month with a statement read, so the screen opens already compared. Over the plan is drawn as a bar at the bucket's right end and said in words, never in red or green: spending over is bad news and saving over is good news, and the screen cannot tell which is which (that is why the comparison panel had no colour either). The bills piece fills the same way, in its grey top line.
  • 5. 50 / 30 / 20 comes back as headings, and that means remembering it. Since M31 U3 the starter makes one bucket per top-level category, so the three buckets are gone. Here the buckets sit under Needs, Wants and Saving, each with its share and its total, on the table and as a bracket above the bar. For that, each bucket remembers which share it was made for (one new column). The starter and Which are these? fill it in; a bucket added later is under Other until you pick Counts as in its menu. A plan made by hand has no shares and no headings. The other way: no headings, and the shares are only the start. Then nothing needs remembering, but the plan no longer reads as 50 / 30 / 20 after the first day.
  • 6. One order for the bar and the table. Needs, then Wants, then Saving, then Other; inside each, the biggest first. The starter puts them in that order once, when it makes them. After that it is the plan's order and it does not move as you drag (re-sorting under your hand would move the divider you are holding). The table follows the bar instead of re-sorting by the category tree, which is what put Savings before Hobbies.
  • 7. Fixed expenses are called bills on Plan too. M31 U1 named them bills on Home and in Plan's suggestions, but the section, the bar and the figures still say fixed expenses. This changes those three. M31 U8 (one name for one thing) can sweep the rest.

Notes for the build

  • Months: a table of month to plan (plan_month: period, plan id), filled from today's applied_to_period by the migration, then that column is no longer read. planInUse reads the table, else the default. editInvalidatesApply goes. Deleting a plan puts its months back on the default. The tool half (D-227) is plan_month taking a list of months to give the plan.
  • The record line is applySuggestions for the month on screen, shown only when the month is on this plan and applied.changes is not empty. Upserting on plan, month and bucket stays, so recording again moves the figure.
  • The fill comes from planAgainstActual (what the comparison panel already reads), passed to PlanBar instead of ticks; the ring fills along its arc by the same rule. VersusPanel and ApplyPanel leave Plan. Draw another plan beside this one stays as it is.
  • Shares: a nullable share on the bucket (needs, wants, saving), written by the starter; sectionBuckets groups by it when any bucket in the plan has one, and otherwise draws no headings.
