One entry view: the page, editable where it stands

Today a row has two views. Clicking it on the sheet or a board opens the entry: every column a heading over a field, with Save at the foot, no links in and none of the page's look. Its page, one button away, has the look you liked, with links out and links in, but it is read only, shows only the text columns, and prints Markdown as raw text. Here they are one view. It starts from the page and you edit any value where it is. Each column type also has more ways to show a value. The three directions differ on layout, on where verdicts and links sit, and on whether the view opens beside the list or in its place. The switches below the directions, Writing and Show as is chosen, are separate choices and work with any direction. Every name and figure is made up.

Show as, per column type

The set proposed for each type, drawn with the same code as the window above. The first way is the default, which is what the sheet's cell already shows. Editing follows from the way: a figure, bar or ring opens a field when pressed; a slider drags; a chip or menu opens the menu; buttons and steps are pressed; a link's name opens its row and the small pencil beside it changes which row. A choice is kept per format, beside the declaration, as today's input is (format_view_column, view entry), so it holds for every entry of that format.

Notes for the build (M35 U2)

  • The page's two settings fold into the entry's. The view page keeps body and line per column, and the development pack declares them in src/data/packs/development/views.md (decision, milestone, open_question and unit). body means Show as Markdown and line means in the header line. U2 maps both onto the entry setting and changes views.md with them. pack.test.ts will notice.
  • Show as replaces the input. Today's ten inputs (inputsFor) are the editing half of the proposed ways. Line, Markdown, Slider, Menu, Buttons and Chips keep their words. Field becomes Figure, Search becomes Name, and Date and Month are the defaults of their types. A stored input word that is no longer offered draws as the default, as it already does.
  • Writing per field, if chosen, is updateRow with the one column changed, through the same coerceRow. A refusal stays in the field with its sentence and writes nothing. Undo is the document's. Parity is unchanged: the writes are the sheet's and the tools'.
  • Verdicts and folds are the row's as stored when writing per field, and the draft's as typed when writing on Save. That second one is today's behaviour (D-140, D-222).
  • Worked-out columns (derivedOf) are on the sheet today and on neither entry view. They are drawn as figures that cannot be edited, marked worked out, with the expression on hover.
  • The header keeps D-296's rule: the title is the first text column shown as a Line, in the view's order.
