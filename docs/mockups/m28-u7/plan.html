Plan: fixed expenses and the split, on one screen

Fixed expenses are the top part. Each one comes out of your net, is a share of the split, or is ignored. Under them, one bar is the split: drag a divider and the buckets either side change. Each bucket keeps its fixed amount, percentage or what's left, and says plainly what it points at. In the window below, drag the dividers (or focus one and press the arrows, Shift for bigger steps), click a bucket's "points at" to change it, and click a choice on a fixed expense.
