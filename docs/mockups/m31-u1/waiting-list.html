For the author to judge

  • 1. Where the list sits. Drawn at the top of Home, above the month's figures, because the figures are wrong until it is answered. The other way: keep it where What wants you is, under What came in, and let the notice bar carry you down to it.
  • 2. The bar on Home. Drawn hidden on Home, since the list says the same thing a few lines lower. The other way: show it everywhere and let Answer them scroll.
  • 3. Two new answers. No pay that month on a missing payslip, and Count the charge as spending on a card with no statement. Without them both questions can only be closed by a document that may never exist. Each is a small write (and a tool half, D-227); say no and they stay Read-only.
  • 4. The order. Everything that changes a total first (settlement, transfers, a card with no statement, a missing statement), then the breakdown (categories), then what the app guessed (payments, bills), then what is missing elsewhere (payslip, fund, holding). It moves transfers and the two missing ones above uncategorized.
  • 5. Three rows per group, biggest first, and Show more opens in place; no group folds away. Uncategorized is by merchant, as By merchant counts them, so its head carries both numbers.

Notes for the build

  • One list, one source. domain/home.ts's attention() grows the kinds it lacks (transfers, a card with no statement, missing statements) and returns rows, not only counts; the notice bar and the list read the same answer, so they cannot disagree.
  • Every answer calls what its screen calls today: setSettlementDecision / decideAllForCard, setTransferDecisions, By merchant's filing, PaymentReview's confirm and dismiss, confirmCandidate, the fund and holding readings. Parity (D-227) holds because the tools already exist; only choice 3's two answers are new writes.
  • A bill is tracked without a plan. confirmCandidate needs none; Plan only hid the candidates until one existed.
  • The queues stay on Settings › Accounts, By merchant, Debts, Plan and Paychecks, unchanged, so an answer is reachable where it is today too.
  • A row answered shows its receipt with Undo until the next answer or a few seconds, then leaves; the counts in the head, the aside and the notice move at once.
