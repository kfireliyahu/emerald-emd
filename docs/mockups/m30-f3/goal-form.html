A goal asks one question: where is the money

The goal form asks Where is the money? and lists the bank accounts, the broker's holdings and the places kept by hand, each by one name. The answer decides what comes next: a bank account asks how much of it is already there; a holding follows the rows that bought it and shows what it is worth; + New… makes a place without leaving the form. The goal's drawer shows the answer and has its own Edit. Every name and figure is made up.

Notes for the build

  • One write. Add goal writes the goal, its place (when + New… made one), the Was already there deposit and the feed in one transaction; Cancel writes nothing. The plugin's create_saving_goals takes the same answer (where, already there), so a conversation does it in one call too (D-227).
  • The answer decides the feed. A holding bought by rows sets fed_by_holding (D-357); an account, a broker's cash or a place kept by hand sets none, and its deposits are typed on the drawer as today. The category in the fold replaces either; never both.
  • Already there is a deposit dated today, into the account's own Cash (D-362), so it counts inside the statement balance once. More than the last statement is a warning, not a refusal: records can be behind.
  • F4 adds one more line under an account's answer: whether transfers arriving there are for this goal. Not drawn here, and it may make the fold's category the rarer choice.
  • The drawer's feed menu goes. What feeds a goal is changed in Edit, which is the same form; the drawer keeps the answer, the cost check and the readings.
  • The Emergency kind leaves the form. A document that has one keeps it as an ordinary goal (D-360); its months fields are not shown.
