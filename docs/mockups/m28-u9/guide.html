Guide me: a small dialog that walks the real screens

No separate onboarding. A new document offers a walk through it; the guide then moves through the ledger's own routes, one place at a time, with a small dialog pinned to the thing it explains: where to drop statements and payslips, By merchant, Plan's fixed expenses and split, and Wealth's opening balance and cushion. Every step can be skipped, the guide can be put away and picked up again, and it is reachable at any time. The steps are a list the pack declares, so the engine can get its own guide later from the same structure.

Everything in the window is live. Press Guide me, then Next, Back, the step strip, or do the thing on the screen itself and watch the step tick. All figures are synthetic.
