In the bank, split between the goals and the cushion

You tick which bank accounts count. Their latest statements are what is in the bank. Each goal saved in one of them holds its part, and the cushion is what is left, held up against its target. When the goals hold more than is there, the card says so and does not shrink anything to make it fit. All figures are made up.

Notes for the build

  • The tick is the account's own. An in_cushion flag on the account, set here and by set_cushion. Only ordinary bank accounts are listed: cards and accounts that hold things never are.
  • A goal's part is its deposits into a ticked account's cash holding (U1), less what it took out. A goal saved somewhere else (a deposit plan, a fund, an unticked account) is not in this card; it stays in "Where the rest is".
  • The check is the whole sum against the whole balance. It fails when the parts add up to more; the cushion then shows "none", never a figure shrunk to zero.
  • The hero's In the bank figure does not change. The card's own total is the ticked accounts only, so the two can differ when an account is unticked.
  • The Emergency goal retires. Setting the months no longer creates it. A document that has one keeps it as an ordinary goal.
