WordCovers exactly

      
        
DashboardAn ordered set of blocks: saved views, rollups’ charts and headline totals, and nothing else. A format’s is its Dashboard tab; the document’s is the rail’s Dashboard, and its blocks may come from any format.

        
BlockOne thing on a dashboard: a saved view drawn compact in its kind, a rollup’s chart, or a headline total. Its title opens it in full.

        
Headline totalOne figure: the count of a format’s rows, or the sum of one of its number or money columns, under a filter as format_rows match takes it, with a name.

        
Add a blockThe menu of what this dashboard may hold: the format’s saved views and rollups not on it yet, and Headline total. On the document’s, grouped by format.

        
Remove from this dashboardTakes the block off. A saved view or a rollup stays where it was kept; a headline total, which lives only on its dashboard, goes.

        
N moreThe rows a compact block does not show. It opens the view in full.
